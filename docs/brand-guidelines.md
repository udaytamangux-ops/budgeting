# Budgeting Brand Guidelines v1.0

> Last updated: 4 August 2026
>
> Status: Provisional product foundation

## Product promise

Budgeting helps young salaried professionals in Nepal understand what remains before month end and make the next spending decision with confidence.

## Personality and voice

- Calm, trustworthy, direct, and respectful.
- Financially precise without sounding like a bank statement.
- Helpful without judgement, hype, or celebration theatre.
- Errors explain what happened and how to recover.
- Success messages state exactly what changed.

## Visual direction

- Inter is the single interface type family.
- Strong numeric hierarchy makes available balance the primary scan target.
- Blue is reserved for primary actions and selection.
- Positive, warning, and danger colours always appear with text or icons.
- White surfaces, subtle borders, and spacing provide hierarchy; shadows are exceptional.
- No gradients, glass effects, decorative charts, or repeated rounded-card containers.

## Component principles

- Primary buttons are at least 48 logical pixels tall.
- Inputs always have visible labels, specific errors, and visible focus states.
- Cards are reserved for tightly related summaries, not every section.
- Confirmation banners are lightweight, dismissible, and actionable.
- All motion is functional, tokenized, brief, and disabled when the operating system requests reduced motion.

## Release governance

The name, application identifiers, icon, bundled Inter assets, and final palette require product approval before release. This document is the implementation source of truth until replaced by an approved brand package.
